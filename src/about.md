<p><mark>I am Brendan Ngwa Nforbi</mark>. This site, www.bookofbrendan.blog, is my book of daily meditations on the Word of God.</p>

<p>Every article here is written by <a href="{{ site.author.path }}">Brendan Ngwa Nforbi</a>. Each morning — or as near to morning as a day allows — I sit with a passage of Scripture and write what I see, what I am convicted of, and what I am asking the Lord to do with it. These are not sermons. They are notes from the chair: short, dated, and meant to be lived.</p>

<p>Read the <a href="/meditations/">meditations</a>, or see every writing collected under <a href="{{ site.author.path }}">Brendan Ngwa Nforbi</a>. Subscribe to the <a href="/feed.xml">RSS feed</a> if you want each new entry in your reader.</p>
