<p><mark>I am Brendan Ngwa Nforbi</mark>. This site is a book of daily meditations on the Word of God.</p>

<p>Each morning — or as near to morning as a day allows — I sit with a passage of Scripture and write what I see, what I am convicted of, and what I am asking the Lord to do with it. These are not sermons. They are notes from the chair: short, dated, and meant to be lived.</p>

<p>If you are here, you are welcome to read along. Subscribe to the <a href="/feed.xml">RSS feed</a> if you want each new entry in your reader. If a meditation helps you pray, that is enough.</p>
